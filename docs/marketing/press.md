# Press release and fact sheet

For Latvian media (LSM, Delfi, TVNET, regional papers, school and Junior
Achievement channels) in the week the app reaches the stores
(docs/11-MARKETING.md §3). The `[brackets]` are filled in on the day; the
founder decides how much of their own story to tell.

Send it as plain text in the email itself, with three screenshots and the
icon attached (`tools/store/screenshots.mjs` makes them, the icon is
`apps/mobile/assets/images/icon.png`), never as the only content of an
attachment.

## Latviski

**Temats:** Skolēns izveidojis ģimenes hronikas lietotni FamStory - bez
reklāmām, ar datiem Eiropā

**[Pilsēta], [datums].** App Store un Google Play šodien parādījusies
latviešu lietotne **FamStory** - privāta ģimenes hronika un kalendārs. Tajā
ģimene glabā savus notikumus, bildes un balss ierakstus, un katrs ieraksts
redzams tikai tiem, kam tas domāts. Lietotni izveidojis [vārds, uzvārds],
[klase] klases skolēns no [skola].

"Mūsu ģimenes bildes bija izkaisītas pa trim čatiem, un vecmāmiņa tās
neredzēja vispār," stāsta [vārds]. "Gribēju vietu, kur ģimenes stāsts paliek
kopā un kur bērnu bildes neaizceļo tālāk par ģimeni."

**Kas FamStory atšķir:**

- **Katrs ieraksts saka, kam tas ir** - visai ģimenei, vecākiem, vecvecākiem
  vai tikai sev. Ieraksti, kuros ir bērns, pēc noklusējuma redzami tikai
  vecākiem.
- **Bērnam nav sava konta.** Bērna profilu pārvalda vecāks.
- **Nav reklāmu, nekas netiek pārdots**, un ģimenes saturs netiek izmantots
  mākslīgā intelekta apmācībai. Bildes un ieraksti glabājas Eiropas Savienībā.
- **Balss ieraksti**: vecvecāki var pastāstīt stāstu, nevis to rakstīt.
- **Laika kapsulas, dzīvesstāsti, gada filma**, dzimšanas dienu atgādinājumi un
  dāvanu idejas pēc tā, kas cilvēkam patīk.

FamStory ir bez maksas. Premium noņem ierobežojumus notikumu, bilžu un balss
ierakstu skaitam.

## In English

**Subject:** A Latvian pupil's family chronicle app, FamStory: no ads, data
kept in Europe

**[City], [date].** FamStory, a private family chronicle and calendar from
Latvia, is out today on the App Store and Google Play. Families keep their
events, photographs and voice notes in it, and every entry is seen only by
the people it is for. It was built by [name], a [grade] pupil at [school].

"Our family's photos were scattered across three chats, and my grandmother
never saw them at all," says [name]. "I wanted one place where the family's
story stays together and the children's photos go no further than the
family."

**What makes it different:**

- **Every entry says who it is for** - the whole family, the parents, the
  grandparents, or only its author. Entries with a child in them are seen by
  the parents only, unless they choose otherwise.
- **A child has no account.** A parent manages the child's profile.
- **No advertising, nothing sold**, and nothing a family writes trains an AI
  model. Photographs and entries are stored in the European Union.
- **Voice notes**: a grandparent can tell a story instead of typing it.
- **Time capsules, life stories, the year as a one-minute film**, birthday
  reminders and gift ideas from what a person likes.

FamStory is free. Premium lifts the limits on events, photographs and voice
notes.

**Contact:** [name], [email], [phone]. The app: [App Store link], [Google Play
link]. Website: https://famstory.lv
