## 1. What FamStory is

A private chronicle and calendar for a family: events, photographs, comments,
reminders and birthdays, each shown only to the people it is meant for.

## 2. Your account

- You need to be **16** or older to have an account of your
  own. Younger children are in FamStory as profiles a parent manages (section 4).
- One account is one person. Keep your password to yourself; you are
  responsible for what is done with your account unless it was used without
  your fault.
- Give a real name the family will recognise. We may ask you to change a name
  that pretends to be somebody else.

## 3. Your family

Whoever creates a family is its first **admin**. Admins invite people, choose
their roles (admin, member, viewer, child profile), manage groups, and can
remove people. A **viewer** can see and react but not add. The family's admins
are responsible for who is in the family; we do not check who is related to
whom.

## 4. Children

A child has **no account** in FamStory. A child profile can only be created by
a member of the family who is the child's parent or legal guardian, or who has
their permission, and every child profile has an adult who manages it. By
creating one you confirm that you have that right.

Entries with a child in them are shown to the parents only unless you
deliberately choose a wider audience. Think before you do; the app will remind
you.

## 5. What you add

**It stays yours.** You own what you write and upload. You give us the
permission we need to provide the service with it — to store it, back it up,
make smaller copies of photographs, and show it to the audience you chose —
and nothing more. That permission ends when the content is deleted, except
for copies in backups, which are overwritten within **35 days**.

**It is part of the family's chronicle.** What you add to a family is shared
with it. If you leave the family or delete your account, what you added stays
in the family's chronicle, attributed to "Former member"; your name,
photograph and birthday are removed. You can delete your own entries before
you leave.

*Note for the lawyer: this is the product's deliberate answer to the
collision between one person's erasure and a family's shared memory
(docs/06-PRIVACY-GDPR.md, "What erasure actually removes"). It needs
checking against Art. 17 for content that is itself personal data about the
person leaving — their own photograph in an event, for example.*

**You need the right to add it.** Only add what you are allowed to share:
your own photographs, and pictures and information about other people when
you may share them with your family. Do not add anything unlawful.

## 6. What you must not do

- try to see what is not meant for you, or get round who may see an entry;
- use somebody else's account, or let others use yours;
- upload anything unlawful, or anything that infringes someone else's rights;
- use FamStory to harass, threaten or spy on anyone;
- interfere with the service — overloading it, probing it for weaknesses,
  copying it by automated means. If you find a security problem, please tell
  us at **{{email.security}}** instead; we will not act against good-faith
  reports.

We may remove content that breaks these terms, and suspend an account that
breaks them seriously or repeatedly — telling you why, and first where we can.

**No tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.** What is
offensive, hateful, sexual, violent or threatening has no place in FamStory,
and neither has anybody who uses it to hurt others.

**Reporting.** If something in your family breaks these terms, open the entry
or the comment and choose *Report*. The family's admins are told, and so are
we. We act on reports within 24 hours: what breaks these terms is removed,
and an account that posted it can be removed from the service.

**Blocking.** You can block any member of your family, on their profile or
from one of their entries. From then on the entries, comments and reactions
they add are not shown to you, and you are not told what they do. Nobody is
told that you blocked them, and you can unblock them on their profile.

*Note for the lawyer: Apple's App Review asks of an app whose people post
things for terms that rule out objectionable content and abusive users,
reporting, blocking, and the developer acting on a report within 24 hours by
removing the content and the user who posted it (guideline 1.2). The 24 hours
is the operator's promise: it needs somebody reading the reports every day.*

## 7. Guest links and calendar feeds

A **guest link** shows one event to whoever holds it, for the time you chose.
A **calendar feed** shows what you can see to whoever holds its address. Both
work like keys: you decide who gets them, and you are responsible for sharing
them only with people who should see what they show. You can withdraw a
guest link and reset a calendar feed at any time.

## 8. Suggestions

FamStory suggests things — an event type, a reminder time — and warns when
somebody already has something at the same time. **Suggestions are a helper,
not advice: they are not legal, medical, psychological or financial advice,
and they may be wrong.** The app never acts on a suggestion by itself; you
decide whether to accept it. For anything about health, a child's wellbeing,
or money, rely on a professional, not on FamStory.

## 9. Plans and payment

FamStory has a free plan with limits (how many events and photographs), and
**Premium**, which removes them. Downloading your own data is free on every
plan and never behind a payment.

**Premium bought in the app** is a subscription through your Apple ID or your
Google Play account:

- The price and the period are shown before you confirm.
- It renews automatically until you turn renewal off, at least 24 hours
  before the period ends.
- You manage or cancel it in the settings of the App Store or Google Play.
- Refunds are Apple's or Google's, under their rules.

The price is the one the App Store or Google Play shows before you confirm,
in your currency and with VAT. Apple and Google sell the subscription to you:
withdrawing from the purchase and refunds are under their terms.

## 10. Availability

We work to keep FamStory running and your data safe — backups every night,
tested each time — but we cannot promise it will never be unavailable, and
during the beta it may change quickly. We will tell you in the app before a
change that takes something away.

## 11. Ending

You can delete your account at any time in Settings; you then have 30 days to
change your mind. We may end the service as a whole with at least **60
days'** notice, during which you can download everything.
